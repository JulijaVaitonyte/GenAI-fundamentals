Hand-picked resources for getting to know generative AI

Welcome! This is a curated list of resources for getting familiar with generative AI, collected in one place. The materials vary in depth, but most assume no background in the field, so you can start wherever you feel comfortable. Everything is organized by topic, so you can work through them in order or jump straight to what interests you. Whether you want to understand how these systems work, what they can and cannot do, or how they affect society, you will find a place to begin. The collection is reviewed regularly.
